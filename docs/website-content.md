## Feature highlights

### 1. VIN scan, vehicle locked in seconds
Scan or type a 17-character VIN and Argos One fills in year, make, model, trim, engine, drivetrain and transmission automatically. No more typing out a full spec sheet before you've even opened the bonnet.

### 2. See what your own shop already fixed
Before you start diagnosing from scratch, Argos One shows you ranked matches from your shop's own repair history for this exact vehicle and complaint — symptoms, what was done, parts used, and how it was verified.

### 3. A repair library that builds itself
Every completed job automatically files itself into a car profile — organised by brand, model and trim. No admin, no manual data entry. Next time that model rolls in, the knowledge is already there.

### 4. Dictate instead of type
Hands dirty, gloves on? Dictate symptoms, observations, work performed and verification notes by voice, then let AI enhance clean the text up — no keyboard required mid-job.

### 5. Know who's allowed to touch what
Technicians can only edit the jobs assigned to them; Owners and Admins can edit anything. Admins can manage their team but can't touch an Owner's account. Role boundaries are enforced in the app, not just written in a policy doc.

### 6. Run multiple sites from one account
Each branch keeps its own jobs, bays, staff and repair library — nothing leaks between sites unless you turn sharing on. Staff can hold a role at more than one branch under a single login, and a shared workshop tablet can be permanently paired to its home branch so nobody has to pick it each morning.

### 7. Share what works — with the network or just your own branches
Anonymised repair patterns flow both ways with the wider Argos One network if you opt in — see the fault and fix other workshops have already found, and contribute yours back. Prefer to keep it in-house? Share named repairs across just your own branches instead, or turn both off entirely.

### 8. Nothing gets lost, nothing's irreversible by accident
Delete a job and it moves to a recoverable Deleted list, not the void — restore it any time, or deliberately delete it forever when you're sure.

## How it works

1. **Scan the VIN, log the complaint.** Vehicle details auto-fill from the VIN; add the customer's symptoms, any DTCs, and arrival photos.
2. **Check for a match.** Argos One surfaces similar repairs your shop has already completed on this exact vehicle before you start from zero.
3. **Fix it, record it.** Log the work performed, parts used, and how you verified the repair — by typing or by voice.
4. **It's in the library forever.** The repair files itself under that car's profile automatically, ready for the next mechanic who sees the same fault.
